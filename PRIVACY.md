# najda — Privacy note

- **Only your circle.** Your position is shared only with the trusted contacts you chose. It's never public, never sold, and never shown on a map to strangers.
- **Only during an alert.** najda does **not** track you continuously. Your location is attached to an alert when one is sent (shake without code, SIGNAL, or SOS) and nowhere else.
- **Deleted when you're safe.** When you tap "Rani labes — I'm safe", the alert's location and address are erased immediately. As a safety net, any location left behind is purged after **7 days** at most.
- **You're in control.** Walk mode (shake detection) is off by default and can be turned off at any time. You can leave a circle or remove a contact whenever you want.
- **Your code stays secret.** Your 4-digit security code is stored only as a one-way hash (SHA-256).
- **Nearby users** (future feature) would receive only an approximate area and the type of emergency, never your identity, unless you choose to share it.
- **Emergency numbers** (197 police, 190 SAMU, 198 civil protection) are dialled by you; najda doesn't send your data to them automatically.
